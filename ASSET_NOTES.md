# Visual correction assets

These two assets were generated using the built-in imagegen tool, then copied into the project. Neither asset contains the interface or Earth globe. The actual DDC logo was extracted directly from the supplied reference and was not generated.

## Text material

Saved project path: `public/assets/distressed-metal.png`.

Prompt: “A seamless square grayscale material texture only, absolutely no text, no letters, no object or scene. Flat scan of distressed metallic silver-white industrial foil, bright off-white base with high contrast irregular charcoal chipped patches, intricate branching black cracks, shattered thin silver leaf, scraped scratches, mottled ink transfer. 75 percent luminous silver-white and 25 percent dark intricate distress. Fine sharp details at multiple scales, no soft cloudy marble and no large geometrical shapes. Fill entire image edge to edge. This will be clipped in real HTML text to reproduce heavily distressed bold DDC lettering.”

## Environmental depth

Saved project path: `public/assets/atmosphere.png`.

Reference input: the user-supplied `Page -1.png`.

Prompt: “Create ONLY an environmental background texture layer for reconstructing the provided approved webpage. Do not generate a webpage. Omit ALL text, all logos, all buttons, all typography, all labels, all planets, the Earth globe, orange orbital lines and bright nodes. Output a widescreen 16:9 dark environmental texture matching just the reference's background: almost black cinematic space with thin irregular fractured charcoal and graphite terrain/smoke streams sweeping diagonally from lower center-left toward upper right behind where a globe would sit. Detailed feathery layered streaks, metallic rocky fibers and sparse dust, subdued gray/silver highlights on some ridges, mostly disappearing into blackness. Leftmost 35 percent mostly near-black and unobtrusive. Middle and lower-right subtly richer in jagged wispy striations. Soft depth haze, photographic fine material detail, not smooth ribbons, not geometric polygons, not vector waves, not obvious wallpaper. Extremely dark restrained lighting. No orange, no glow balls, no recognizable objects. This is an isolated subordinate atmosphere asset only, to sit behind real 3D globe and HTML interface.”
